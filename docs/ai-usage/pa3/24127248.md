## Log 01 – Designing and Reviewing the C4 Container Diagram

**Tool:** ChatGPT  
**Part:** PA3 – Software Architecture: C4 Level 2 – Container Diagram  
**Asked for:** I asked ChatGPT to design a C4 Level 2 diagram for ImpactHub based on the previous PA documents, ensuring that the architecture remained consistent with the established requirements and project scope.  
**AI response:** ChatGPT proposed a three-tier architecture consisting of a React web application, a Node.js/Express REST API, and a PostgreSQL database. It also included Cloudinary as an external image storage service and grouped email notifications and sandbox payment services into an optional external component. However, the proposed diagram grouped several actors together and did not clearly describe their individual interactions with the system.  
**Kept:** I decided to retain the three main containers because they were consistent with the technology stack established in PA1 and the implementation constraints in PA2. This separation also avoids introducing unnecessary deployment complexity for the five Must-have features.  
**Changed:** I identified the need to separate the actors according to their responsibilities and access permissions rather than grouping Guest with Registered User or Donor with Organizer. The relationships should also describe their purposes and communication protocols instead of showing only generic connections between containers.  
**Rejected:** I rejected the proposed grouping of email notifications and sandbox payment services into a single external system because they represent independent integrations with different priorities. In particular, a payment gateway is not a required dependency of the MVP because simulated donations are processed through an internal demo wallet. I also rejected treating individual functional modules as independent containers, since this would misrepresent the selected layered monolithic architecture.  
**Verification:** I used PA2 Sections 2.1, 3.4, 3.5, 5.1, and 6 as references for evaluating the proposed architecture, particularly the five Must-have features, technology constraints, actor permissions, and simulated donation requirements. The final diagram still needs to be checked against the PA1 System Context Diagram and the PA3 architectural decisions, component diagram, and deployment diagram before submission.
